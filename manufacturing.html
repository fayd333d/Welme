Manufacturing

Made in the USA with an experienced global supplement partner

Welme supplements are produced and packaged in partnership with SIRIO Americas, part of SIRIO Pharma, a global dietary supplement manufacturer with more than 30 years of experience in developing and producing nutritional products. SIRIO operates manufacturing capabilities in the United States through its California-based facilities.

For Welme, this means working with an established manufacturing partner with expertise across a broad range of supplement formats, including capsules, tablets, softgels, powders and gummies.

From supplements to your daily packs

Welme was created to make taking supplements simpler. Instead of managing multiple bottles and trying to remember what to take and when, your supplements are organised into convenient daily packs based on your Welme plan.

Our manufacturing and packaging partnership with SIRIO Americas helps us bring this system to life, combining supplement production with professional packaging capabilities designed for consistency, quality and everyday convenience. SIRIO provides end-to-end contract manufacturing services that can include formulation, production and packaging.

Quality at every step

SIRIO is an established global contract development and manufacturing organisation specialising in dietary supplements. Its expertise spans product development, manufacturing, testing and packaging, supported by dedicated research, quality and production capabilities.

By partnering with an experienced supplement manufacturer, Welme can focus on what makes our system different: making supplements easier to understand, easier to organise and easier to fit into everyday life.

Made for your plan. Packed for your routine.

Every Welme box brings your supplements together in one place, organised into simple morning and night packs to help remove the guesswork from your daily routine.
