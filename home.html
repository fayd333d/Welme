COMMON QUESTIONS
How does the personalised recommendation work?+

You answer a short quiz about your age, lifestyle, diet and goals. Our system matches your answers to supplement combinations grounded in clinical research, and your plan is reviewed before your first box ships.

Is your packaging eco friendly?+

Yes. The box and daily cards are recyclable paperboard, and the sachets use minimal-material film – far less waste than a shelf of plastic bottles.

When do I feel the benefits?+

Some people notice better sleep and energy within the first weeks. Most benefits build gradually – we recommend giving your plan a full 8–12 weeks.

Where do you ship?+

We currently ship everywhere in the United States. Delivery is free, every month.

How does pricing work?+

You create a plan, and the price is calculated from what goes into it. Most plans start from about $1 per day, and you can adjust your plan any time to fit your budget.
