Built with supplement science experts

Welme works with NaturPro Scientific, an independent dietary supplement consultancy. Their expertise helped us develop the scientific framework behind our recommendation system, including how supplements are assessed, selected, combined, and explained.

How the Welme framework works

Your answers are processed through a structured system developed with expert input. The framework considers your personal information, selected health goals, and additional concerns to identify supplements that may be relevant to you.

It helps define:

  • which ingredients can be recommended for each goal;
  • appropriate dosage ranges;
  • how supplements can be combined;
  • when to take your daily pack for best absorption;
  • important safety rules and exclusions;
  • how each recommendation should be explained.

This means your plan is built from a carefully defined supplement matrix rather than an unrestricted list of products or generic wellness advice.

Clear and responsible recommendations

Each supplement in your plan is connected to the information you provide. We explain why it was selected, how it may support your goals, and how it fits into your daily routine.

We aim to avoid unnecessary ingredients, unsupported promises, and confusing scientific language. As the Welme system evolves, its framework and recommendation rules can be reviewed and refined with specialist input.

Important information

NaturPro Scientific helped develop the framework used by Welme. They do not individually review every customer protocol before it is sent.

Welme provides personalised wellness information and does not diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent medical conditions. Our recommendations do not replace advice from a physician or another qualified healthcare professional. Speak with your healthcare provider before taking supplements, especially if you take medication, are pregnant or breastfeeding, have a medical condition, or are preparing for surgery.
